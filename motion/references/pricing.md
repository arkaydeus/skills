# Motion pricing

Sell a timed, pixel-true product demo with source. Do not sell "AI video".

British English throughout (colour, organised, towards, artefact).

Default technical scope for a product reel is in `SKILL.md` (job routing) and `references/export.md` (masters). Keep `references/harness.md` in mind when you estimate agent time.

## Caveat: the published numbers disagree

Source write-ups mix several ranges without reconciling them:

- A title aimed at a **$500** reel
- A heading of **$300–$1,000** client contracts
- Listed briefs at **$300**, **$500**, and **$2,000**
- Traditional motion-agency comparisons of **$1,500–$5,000** for a 15-second B2B reel on a two-week turnaround
- One worked example of **$450** revenue against about **$4.80** inference and 45 minutes of engineer time

Those figures were not independently verified here, and some came from marketplace listings that may have closed. **Do not copy them as a rate card.** Quote from scope (duration, aspects, revisions, source) and from your own calendar. Use the anecdotes only as a sense of demand: short interface demos are already being bought in the low hundreds to low thousands.

## What to pitch

Never lead with the model. Lead with the artefact:

> A 15-second interactive product demo of your core workflow, pixel-true typography, clean source, 16:9 master plus 9:16 for ads.

Demand sits with SaaS founders and DTC brands who need a landing-page hero, a launch clip, or paid social. They already know agency prices and timelines.

## Lock the boundary

Write these into the statement of work. If the client wants more, that is a new quote.

| Default | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Interactions | Exactly one core interaction |
| Runtime | 15 seconds |
| Deliverables | 16:9 master, 9:16 cut, source repo |
| Revisions | Two rounds |
| Not included | Extra features, live-action, licensed music, unlimited variants |

A concept demo you were not asked for is still the best closer: pick a public product in the niche, animate one interaction (three cards into one view is the stock example), label it **Concept Demo**. No NDA, and the portfolio is specific.

## A worked unit (illustrative)

Replace the money line with your quote. Keep the cost structure honest.

| Line | Example snapshot |
| --- | --- |
| Client fee | $450 in the source example; quote in the client's currency |
| Inference | a few dollars, not hundreds, if the workspace fence holds |
| Local render | $0 on a laptop |
| Engineer time | about 45 minutes to a couple of hours including two critique rounds |

If the harness does not hold, time goes to overnight retries and the hourly rate collapses. Price the round cap, not the best case.

## Close

1. Outcome sentence, not tool list.
2. Concept demo already on disk.
3. Written boundary: one interaction, 15 seconds, two masters, two rounds.

Launch checklist for the studio itself (once): Node, ffmpeg, Playwright Chromium, `render.mjs`, `motion.js`, the folder fence, a 15-second sample run through two critique rounds, then publish the sample on X and LinkedIn with a short code excerpt. Credit Florence and damkin when the piece used this method.
